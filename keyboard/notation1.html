Task 1 - ‘The Well-Tempered Clavier’ by JS Bach

The Well-Tempered Clavier is a famous collection of keyboard music written by Johann Sebastian Bach in the 1700s. Bach wrote it to show that keyboard instruments could play music in every key and still sound good. Bach wrote two books, each containing 24 preludes and 24 fugues. A prelude is usually smooth and flowing, while a fugue is more complex, with melodies copying and answering each other. This means there are 48 pieces in total. The music is important because Bach uses many compositional techniques. He creates strong melodies and uses harmony to build tension and release.
