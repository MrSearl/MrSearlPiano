Task 3 - ‘Clair de Lune’ by Claude Debussy

Claude Debussy's Suite bergamasque is one of his most famous piano works. It was first written around 1890 and revised before being published in 1905. The third movement, Clair de lune, is the best known. Its title means "moonlight" in French and was inspired by a poem by Paul Verlaine. Written in D-flat major, the piece has a calm and dreamy mood. Debussy uses smooth melodies, rich harmonies and changes in dynamics to create a peaceful atmosphere. The music does not have a strong beat, giving it a flowing and expressive feel. The tempo changes slightly throughout the piece, adding emotion and contrast. Clair de lune has appeared in many films and TV shows and is often used to suggest beauty, sadness and reflection.
