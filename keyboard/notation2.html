Task 2 - ‘Für Elise’ by Ludwig van Beethoven

Bagatelle No. 25 in A minor for solo piano, commonly known as Für Elise, is one of Ludwig van Beethoven's most famous compositions. It was discovered and published 40 years after his death. The piece is written for solo piano and begins with a famous melody recognised around the world. *Für Elise* is mainly in A minor, giving the music a sad and thoughtful mood. The main theme returns several times, with contrasting sections in between, creating an ABA structure. Beethoven uses changes in dynamics and tempo to add expression and contrast.
