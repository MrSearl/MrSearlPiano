Task 3 - ‘Can’t You See What You’re Doing To Me’ by Ana Popovic

Ana Popović is a Serbian blues guitarist and singer who was born in 1976 in Belgrade. She was introduced to the blues by her father and began playing guitar at the age of fifteen.

She is known for her expressive guitar playing and her ability to combine blues with styles such as soul and funk. Her music often features powerful vocals, energetic rhythms and impressive guitar solos.

Popović uses techniques such as string bends, vibrato and improvisation to create emotion and excitement in her performances.

Known for her blend of electric funk, slide guitar, and jazzy instrumentals, Ana Popovic continues to inspire audiences worldwide with her powerful performances and dedication to the blues genre.
